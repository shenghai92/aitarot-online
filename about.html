About and editorial standards

About Love AI Tarot: clear guidance, stated limits

Love AI Tarot is built for people who want a calmer way to reflect on love, career, and change. We aim to be useful without turning uncertainty into a sales tactic or presenting symbolic guidance as proof.

Published by: Love AI Tarot Editorial Team Last reviewed: August 17, 2026 Contact: support@aitarot.online

What this page covers: who publishes the site, how guide pages are reviewed, how the AI Tarot method is explained, and where to find our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What Love AI Tarot is—and what it is not

Love AI Tarot is a privacy-first reading and education site covering Tarot, relationship questions, career decisions, and optional timing context from astrology, Bazi, and the I Ching. The guides are designed to help users frame a question, notice a pattern, and choose a sensible next step.

The site is not a medical, legal, financial, employment, or crisis-support service. A reading cannot verify another person's private thoughts, force an outcome, replace a contract review, or make a high-stakes decision safe. When a question depends on evidence or qualified support, the page should say so plainly.

Our editorial standards

The Love AI Tarot Editorial Team is the publishing standard used across the site's guides and explanatory pages. The purpose of this label is accountability: readers should know that the content is meant to be reviewed for clarity, method, safety, and usefulness, rather than treated as an anonymous stream of confident predictions.

Clarity over certainty

We do not promise that a person will text, an employer will hire, or a particular future is guaranteed. Questions are rewritten toward patterns, choices, and observable next steps.

Method before mystery

Pages should explain the spread, the position of each card, and the difference between symbolic interpretation and factual information. See how AI Tarot works.

Context without overreach

Love, no-contact, career, and beginner pages use different examples and boundaries. We avoid using one generic paragraph to answer every kind of life question.

Privacy by default

Names and birth details are optional for general readings. Users should not need to share passwords, confidential work material, medical records, or another person's private messages.

How we review a guide page

  1. Define the reader's question. The opening should answer the actual search intent instead of starting with a vague promise.
  2. Explain the method. A spread needs named positions, a clear use case, and an honest description of what the system can produce.
  3. Check the boundaries. Relationship pages should respect consent and free will; career pages should separate reflection from hiring, financial, or legal advice.
  4. Make the next step usable. A reader should leave with a question, conversation, preparation task, or boundary—not only an invitation to draw again.
  5. Review links and freshness. Related links should lead to a genuinely different purpose, and important method or policy changes should be reflected in the page date.

How AI Tarot content is produced

The site's reading experience may use software to select cards and generate a readable interpretation from the selected focus, question, spread, and optional context. That output is not psychic sensing and does not provide access to another person's mind. The AI Tarot transparency guide explains the typical stages, useful quality checks, privacy questions, and the difference between AI and a human reader.

Educational pages are written to explain the method and its limits. They are not presented as independent scientific proof of Tarot, astrology, Bazi, or I Ching. Readers are free to disagree with a symbolic interpretation and should return to direct evidence, personal values, and qualified support when the decision is consequential.

Privacy and support

The product keeps a lower-data path available: one-time readings do not require an account, names are optional, and birth details are optional unless a user wants additional timing context. Memberships require account access because they provide returning permissions. The Privacy Policy explains what may be processed, why it is used, and how to contact support about an applicable request.

If a page is unclear, outdated, or makes a claim that does not match these standards, contact support@aitarot.online. Please include the page URL and the specific passage so it can be reviewed. We will not ask you to send sensitive personal details just to report a content issue.
