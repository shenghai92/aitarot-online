Privacy Policy

How privacy works on Love AI Tarot

Love AI Tarot is designed for vulnerable moments. Many people arrive with questions about relationships, silence, heartbreak, work stress, or future uncertainty. That is exactly why privacy matters here. We aim to collect only what is required to deliver the service and avoid turning sensitive questions into unnecessary personal profiling.

Published by: Love AI Tarot Editorial Team Last reviewed: 2026-08-19 Editorial standards

Policy note: This page describes current data practices. If a material change affects collection, access, or deletion, the effective date will be updated here.

Effective date

Effective date: 2026-05-29.

Editorial responsibility for this policy

Privacy claims should be specific enough to check. This page distinguishes required service data from optional details, explains why recurring access needs an account, and links to support for questions about applicable requests. It does not promise that an online service has no logs or third-party processing; the goal is to describe the current product boundary honestly.

What we collect

We may process basic access logs, the question content you submit, optional profile fields such as name or birth details, and required payment-status information for service delivery. Payment processing is handled through Creem. Membership access may also involve account email and access-session data because recurring plans require returning login access.

What not to submit for a general reading

You do not need to submit passwords, private chat transcripts, medical records, financial account details, employer secrets, customer information, or another person’s identifying details for a general reflection. If a question can be asked without a name, exact birth time, or private message, use the lower-detail version. This reduces unnecessary exposure without claiming that an online service has no logs or third-party processing.

How to check a privacy claim

Look for a clear explanation of required fields, optional fields, analytics, payment providers, retention, deletion requests, and support contact. “Private” and “anonymous” should not be treated as absolute words unless the actual service boundary is explained. For general consumer guidance on online information sharing, see the FTC privacy guidance.

What is optional

Name is optional. Birth date and birth time are optional. One-time paid readings do not require login. This matters because not every user wants account-based usage for a private emotional question. We intentionally preserve a lower-data path for free and one-time reading formats.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not treat emotional reading questions as unrelated ad-profiling material. We do not require more personal information than is necessary for the selected reading or membership path.

How data is used

Submitted information is used to generate and deliver the requested reading, verify payment or member access when needed, improve service reliability, and respond to support requests. We do not claim that optional birth details are required for every reading type. They are included only because some users want astrology or Bazi-style timing layers.

Your rights

You may request export or deletion of identifiable session data where applicable. If you need account or privacy help, contact support@aitarot.online. We will review the request based on the data necessary to provide the service and comply with applicable obligations.

How information moves through a reading

A useful privacy explanation follows the user journey. Before submission, decide whether the question can be answered with less detail. During the request, the service may process the question and selected context to generate or deliver the requested experience. If a payment or membership path is used, account and payment-status information may be needed for access. Afterward, support or applicable requests may require enough information to identify the relevant session or account.

This page does not promise a universal retention period or that no provider, log, or infrastructure layer exists. Those details depend on the current product path and applicable obligations. If you need a specific access or deletion review, contact support without sending the sensitive story itself.

What deletion and correction requests can mean

A request may involve identifying the relevant account or session, confirming that the request is yours, and distinguishing data needed for a current transaction from data that can be removed. We will review requests based on the actual service path and applicable obligations. Do not email passwords, payment credentials, medical records, or full private conversations to request privacy help.

Third-party resources and external links

The site may link to payment, analytics, font, hosting, or authoritative reference services. A link is not a claim that another service follows this policy. Review the destination’s own privacy information before submitting data there. Browser privacy controls and device security also matter; a site cannot make a user’s device, network, or third-party account private by wording alone.

Policy updates

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the site after an update means the revised policy applies to future use.
